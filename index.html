How to play

✈️ Drag anywhere to fly. Your guns fire automatically. On a computer you can also use the arrow keys or WASD.

💣 Bombs drop automatically on ships, subs and island targets lined up ahead of you. You can also tap BOMB (or press B).

💥 MEGA clears every enemy bullet and blasts everything on screen (Space).

🚢 Ship hulls are tough against guns, so bomb them! 🫧 Dived subs can only be hit by bombs.

🎈 Shoot the yellow cargo planes for power-ups: P power, S shield, W wingmen, + repair, M magnet, B mega bomb.

🛡️ Bosses have armor: knock out their turrets first, then sink the core!

⭐ Earn 3 stars: finish the mission, shoot down 70% of enemies, and keep at least half your health. You keep your coins even if you crash!

About Skyfire Squadron

Skyfire Squadron is a bright cartoon airplane shooter. Your little plane fires by itself while you steer through waves of enemy fighters, drop bombs on ships and collect power-ups.

Drag anywhere to steer. Missions open one by one and are scored with three stars. Coins buy five planes and six upgrades in the Hangar, and bosses wait in all four stages.

How to play

Drag anywhere to steer: the plane moves with your finger, so your thumb never covers it. Your guns shoot automatically. Tap BOMB to drop a bomb on ships and island guns, and tap MEGA for an emergency flash that clears every enemy bullet. On a keyboard, B drops a bomb.

Tips from Zaib

  1. Stay near the bottom of the screen. It gives you the most time to see bullets coming.
  2. Make small moves. A short slide sideways is usually enough to dodge.
  3. Use bombs on ships. Gunfire works, but bombs finish ships faster.

Good to know

The enemies are cartoon planes and ships, and nobody gets hurt. Your progress is saved on your device.

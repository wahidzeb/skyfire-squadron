How to play

✈️ Drag anywhere to fly. Your guns fire automatically. On a computer you can also use the arrow keys or WASD.

💣 Bombs drop automatically on ships, subs and island targets lined up ahead of you. You can also tap BOMB (or press B).

💥 MEGA clears every enemy bullet and blasts everything on screen (Space).

🚢 Ship hulls are tough against guns, so bomb them! 🫧 Dived subs can only be hit by bombs.

🎈 Shoot the yellow cargo planes for power-ups: P power, S shield, W wingmen, + repair, M magnet, B mega bomb.

🛡️ Bosses have armor: knock out their turrets first, then sink the core!

⭐ Earn 3 stars: finish the mission, shoot down 70% of enemies, and keep at least half your health. You keep your coins even if you crash!
